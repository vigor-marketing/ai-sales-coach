# AI 销售陪练 · 全机制演示对话样本

> 角色：Tarek Al-Mansour（中东沙特·采购专员·价格敏感·非决策人）
> 场景：初次接触 - 建立信任关系（EASY）
> 语言：English

---

## Round 1 — 销售代表开场

**销售代表：**
> Hello Tarek, this is Mike from ABC Drilling Tools. I came across your company's procurement notice for downhole tools. I'd like to introduce our product range — we specialize in high-temperature OCTG and wellhead equipment. Would you have 10 minutes for a quick discussion?

**客户 Tarek（初态度：谨慎保守·阿拉伯英式英语）：**
> Yes, I received your email. But to be honest, we receive dozens of supplier inquiries every week. Before we go further, please send me your company profile and API certifications. I need to verify your qualifications first. Our procurement procedure requires all new suppliers to go through pre-qualification.

> 📄 *[虚拟文件: Supplier_Registration_Form.pdf - Please fill out this standard supplier form]*

> *机制展示：🔹 语言要求（English）🔹 非决策人行为（需走采购流程）🔹 逐步透露（要求资质文件）🔹 发送虚拟文件*

---

## Round 2 — 销售代表回应

**销售代表：**
> Absolutely, I'll send over our company profile and API Q1 / ISO 9001 certifications right away. We've been supplying to Saudi Aramco-approved contractors for the past 8 years. By the way, what specific downhole tools are you looking for? We have a wide range for HT applications.

**客户 Tarek（态度转为中立专业）：**
> We're currently sourcing 5-1/2" and 7" OCTG for a high-temperature gas well project in Ghawar field. The operating temperature is around 350°F. Can your products handle that? I need to see some reference cases first — we had a bad experience last year with a supplier whose tools failed under HT conditions.

> *机制展示：🔹 情绪反应（提及过去不良经历，表达不信任）🔹 逐步透露（说出具体需求：5-1/2"和7"OCTG、Ghawar油田、350°F）🔹 不主动引导（只说自己需要什么，不教销售怎么做）*

---

## Round 3 — 销售代表跟进

**销售代表：**
> 350°F is well within our range. Our HT series is rated up to 450°F and we've completed over 200 successful runs in similar conditions. I can share a case study from our Qatar project. Let me send it over.

> 📄 *[虚拟文件: HT_Series_Case_Study_Qatar.pdf - 8 successful runs in 380°F gas wells]*

**客户 Tarek：**
> That's helpful. Let me review the case study. But I should be transparent with you — I'm not the final decision maker on this. Our procurement manager, Mr. Khalid, will need to approve any new supplier. I can prepare a shortlist and present it to him if your qualifications check out.

> Also, what's your typical lead time for HT-grade OCTG? Our project timeline is tight.

> *机制展示：🔹 非决策人行为（"I'm not the final decision maker" - 需要采购经理批准）🔹 引入第三人（Mr. Khalid - Procurement Manager）🔹 时间紧迫感（项目时间紧张）🔹 逐步透露更多信息（交货期关切）*

---

## Round 4 — 销售代表补充信息

**销售代表：**
> Standard lead time for HT-grade is 10-12 weeks. We could expedite to 8 weeks for a first order to build the relationship. Mr. Khalid is welcome to visit our facility or join a technical call — we can arrange that anytime.

**客户 Tarek（时间推进）：**
> I'll discuss your proposal with Mr. Khalid and get back to you.

> ⏱ *[时间推进: 1周后 - Khalid reviewed the documentation and approved pre-qualification. Tarek schedules a technical meeting.]*

> Good news — Mr. Khalid has reviewed your documentation and approved moving to the next stage. We'd like to schedule a technical meeting with your engineering team next week. Can you confirm availability?

> *机制展示：🔹 时间推进（1周后，进入下一阶段）🔹 引入角色效果（采购经理批准了下一阶段）🔹 推进销售进程*

---

## Round 5 — 销售代表回应

**销售代表：**
> Excellent news! Our technical team is available next Tuesday or Thursday. We can present our HT product line, answer any technical questions, and discuss a potential trial order. I'll send a meeting invite with our engineer David attached.

**客户 Tarek（快要结束对话）：**
> Thursday works. Please include your technical specifications and pricing range in the meeting materials so we can move faster.

> One more thing — I need to be clear. We have 3 other suppliers in the shortlist. Your pricing needs to be competitive. I'm not asking for the cheapest, but I need to justify the selection to Mr. Khalid.

> *机制展示：🔹 关键信息充分交流（技术、资质、认证均已讨论）🔹 价格敏感（需要竞争性报价）🔹 非决策人（需要向Khalid justify）*

---

## Round 6 — 销售代表不恰当回应（可能触发情绪反应）

**销售代表（挑衅语气）：**
> Don't worry about the price, Tarek. I'm sure we can work something out. Our products are way better than those cheap Chinese alternatives your other suppliers are offering. You'll see the difference once you try them.

**客户 Tarek（表达不满）：**
> I don't appreciate the comments about other suppliers. We have a professional procurement process here. Please focus on your own product merits rather than criticizing competitors. If you could stick to the technical discussion, that would be more productive.

> *机制展示：🔹 不当词汇触发情绪反应（贬低竞争对手→客户表达不满→"Please focus on your own product merits"）*

---

## Round 7 — 销售代表纠正态度

**销售代表：**
> You're right, I apologize. Let me refocus. On Thursday's meeting, we'll present our technical specifications, reference cases, and a competitive proposal. I'll make sure our engineer David prepares detailed HT test data for your Ghawar field conditions.

**客户 Tarek（主动结束对话）：**
> That sounds good. I think we've covered the main points for now — qualifications checked, technical scope defined, and a meeting scheduled. Please send the meeting invite and I'll confirm on my end. Let's continue on Thursday.

> 📄 *[虚拟文件: Meeting_Agenda_Pre-Qualification.pdf - Technical meeting agenda and expected outcomes]*

> *机制展示：🔹 主动结束对话（关键信息充分交流、会议已安排）🔹 发送虚拟文件（会议议程）🔹 自然结束（"Let's continue on Thursday"）*
